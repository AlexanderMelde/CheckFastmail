# Privacy Policy for Checker for Fastmail

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## Overview
Checker for Fastmail ("the Extension") is an open-source Chrome Extension designed to provide quick, lightweight access to your Fastmail unread emails. We take your privacy extremely seriously. 

Our core principle is simple: **Zero external data collection.**

## Data We Collect
**None.** The Extension does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your data to any third parties. We operate with zero backend servers and zero analytics or telemetry scripts.

## How Your Data is Handled
All data processing and communication happens directly and strictly between your browser and Fastmail's official servers (`api.fastmail.com`).

- **Authentication:** The Extension uses a Fastmail Personal API Token generated by you. The token is stored securely and solely within your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). It is never synced to external devices or transmitted to any server other than Fastmail's official API (`https://api.fastmail.com`).
- **Emails:** Unread email headers and previews fetched from Fastmail are stored temporarily in browser memory while the extension popup is open. Email bodies are rendered inside an isolated, sandboxed frame. Data is not permanently stored or logged anywhere.
- **Permissions:** 
  - `storage`: Used solely to save your Fastmail API token and session endpoints locally on your device.
  - `alarms`: Used to schedule a low-frequency background poll of the Fastmail API (every 5 minutes) to update your unread count badge.

## Changes to This Policy
If any changes are made to this privacy policy in the future, they will be reflected here and explicitly stated in the extension's release notes.

## Contact
